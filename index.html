Unknown to known, on orbit.

Penumbra Autonomy builds onboard perception, estimation and navigation that let spacecraft see, characterize and operate around uncooperative objects, without waiting on the ground.

Our capabilities

See the target.
Understand it.
Operate around it.

Perception, estimation and navigation for inspection and proximity operations around uncooperative objects.

01Perception

Detect and track through changing illumination.

Maintain awareness of an object as it moves through light and shadow. Our perception capabilities support inspection of targets that offer no cooperative assistance.

A clearer view of the object your mission depends on.

02Estimation

Characterize the target and its motion.

Build an understanding of an object's behavior and its state relative to the observing spacecraft, informing inspection and approach decisions.

More knowledge before committing to closer operations.

03Navigation

Support proximity operations onboard.

Provide relative navigation around uncooperative objects, supporting spacecraft operations as conditions change and reducing reliance on continuous ground involvement.

Greater autonomy for operations around unfamiliar targets.

04Scalability

Low-SWaP-C autonomy.

Designed for spacecraft with tight size, weight, power, and cost constraints.

Accessible autonomy across spacecraft and fleets.
